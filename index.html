What we do

Heji Connect is a financial data aggregation and reporting company. We serve business owners, finance teams, and the advisors who work with them.

Data aggregation

We connect, with your authorization, to bank accounts, accounting software, payment processors, and payroll systems, and bring the data into one consistent view.

Business health reporting

Scheduled and on-demand reports on cash position, revenue trends, receivables, expenses, and runway, written in plain language.

Audit-ready records

Every number keeps a record of where it came from and when it changed, so you can share reports with lenders, investors, or auditors with confidence.

How it works

  1. Connect

    You authorize read-only access to the financial sources you choose. You can revoke access at any time.

  2. Reconcile

    We normalize and cross-check records across sources and flag anything that doesn't match.

  3. Report

    You receive reports showing each figure alongside the source records behind it.

  4. Share

    Export or share a read-only report with the people you choose, with its full history attached.

Security and trust

Your financial data is sensitive, and we handle it that way. We use read-only connections wherever possible and never move money on your behalf.

Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Access control. Only authorized users on your account can view your reports.

No data sales. We do not sell your financial data or your personal information to anyone.

Your control. Disconnect a source or request deletion of your data at any time.

Text message notifications

Customers can choose to receive text messages from Heji Connect about their account. Messages include report-ready notices, alerts when a financial figure needs review, and one-time verification codes for signing in.

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box when you create an account or submit the contact form below, or by enabling text notifications in your account settings. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency varies by account activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help. You can also contact us at support@hejiconnect.com. Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
